# The four critiques: what to build first (2026-11-25)

Ranked by gain for the work (S half a day, M one to two days, L more); details in critique-drag-race.md, critique-padel.md, critique-boat-race.md, critique-night-quest.md.

1. **Drag: a real Tadek and shifts that matter (S + M).** Today a tap at the first yellow beats him by 1.2 s and the nitro by 3.9 s; perfect timing is worth 0.2 s. Five gears with a pull each, a 0.12 s green, a bog for short shifts, one bottle, Tadek on the same rules with four nights.
2. **Padel: the swing (M).** Today a tap before the ball arrives can never score above 0.45, and the hint teaches exactly that tap. The swing commits to the best contact, a ring closes on the ball, CZYSTO is a 5-frame window, timing aims the ball.
3. **Boat: the inside buoy, the rhythm meter, banks that scrub, a record (four S).** Today full gas with centre aim misses no ring, even at 1.5 times the speed, and half a second on a bank is a dead stop. No re-bake needed.
4. **Night: the pot and the night bar (S).** One sum fed by every beat, doubled or halved by the drag, and five pips that say where the night is. Build after 1, or the double is free money.
5. **Padel: your lane gets the ball, shorter pauses, a short decider (S).** You play a quarter of the pair's balls today; the target is half, in 75 to 120 s.
6. **Night: the race shows gaps by name and warms your tyres for the drag (S).** The best beat starts to count for the finale.
7. **Drag: Tadek's tells, the gap bar, hit-stop, the photo finish (four S).** You see his mistakes and the fight, not only the card.
8. **Night: a raid with a siren tell, a wide gap and two cordon cars that chase (M).** The raid stops being a 4.5 s timer.
9. **Padel: pressure you can watch in place of the hidden roll (M).** Rivals lose points on late feet, after your corner or smash. Needs the 24 seeds re-tuned.
10. **Boat: four hard gates and a slide (M, a course re-bake).** The first use for the brake.
11. **Night: four nights with card levels and new lines; raid 2 as a searchlight (S + M).** A reason for night two.
12. **Boat: restart from the crossing, a pack that waits out of sight (M).** For the player who is 4th of 4 today.
13. Later: the aimed serve, the span to choose under Poniatowski, Lena's lane, racers getting caught, meets that move, your own donut (waits for the drift quest).

Order of PRs: 1 and 7 (drag), then 2 and 5 (padel), then 3 (boat), then 4 and 6 (night); each ends with an Autopilot row (padel has none, the night chain has never run without teleports).
F decides: raid 2 before or after the drag, no bottle on night 1, the padel stick as aim only, the pot halved when caught. Only the phone shows: a 0.12 s shift and a 5-frame padel hit under a thumb, a 3.5 m buoy band at 145 km/h, the meet's frame time.
