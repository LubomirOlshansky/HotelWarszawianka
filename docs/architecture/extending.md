# Extending the game: where each change goes

Read this before adding anything. Each row names the one owner. If the owner is marked
**planned**, it doesn't exist yet: do what the "Until then" column says, so the change is easy to
move later, and don't build a third private copy.

## The table

| You are adding | Plug into | Don't |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A line of text on the HUD (toast, ticker, note) | `hud.toast` / `ticker` / `show_note` with a priority from `messages.gd` (PR #652: AMBIENT, RADIO, INFO, QUEST_LINE, ALERT) | own cooldown or own "is the player busy" test |
| A hint for a new player (eight words, once, pointing at a control) | `hints.gd`: an id in `IDS` and `TARGETS`, its words in `data/tips.json` `hints`, its rule in `decide` and its end in `done_now`, the fact it needs in main.gd `_hints_frame`; on screen hud.gd `spotlight(target, words)` (a new target is a case in `spotlight_target_rect`); a hint inside the full map is an id in `MAP_IDS` with its rule in `decide_map` and its end in `map_done` (full_map.gd draws it; the game is paused there, so no spotlight); a loading tip is one line in `data/tips.json` `tips` with the `tag` of what it tells about | a toast of your own for "how to", a hint shown from your own timer or one that shows again every session |
| A speech bubble | `bubbles.say` (QUEST), `say_reaction`, `say_ambient`; police use the POLICE class | a timer that decides when a character may talk |
| An incoming phone call | `hud.call_ok()` before ringing (PR #652), `messages.call_rang()` when it rings (`show_call` and an ODBIERZ card record it themselves) (the rescue line too: missions.gd waits at zero until it may ring) | ringing from your own timer without asking |
| Dialogue words | `game/data/dialogue.json` (Polish and English pair), read with `dialogue.line(group, tag)`; branching scenes in Dialogue Manager (skill `dialogue-scenes`) | text inside code |
| A new activity (quest, job, race mode, tour) | **the Activity kit** (TD-1): a script that `extends "res://scripts/activity.gd"`, gives the words (`_call_card`, `_offer_card`) and the play (`_begin`, `_run_tick`, `_card_pressed`), and uses `set_target`, `show_card`, `status`, `finish` and `drop`. Optional hooks: `_tick` (every frame, in every phase: a timer of its own for the next call), `_refused` (the call or the offer was turned down or not answered), `_ended`. A card can close by itself (`show_card(..., life)`: that counts as a press of its first button, unless the script gives its own `_card_expired()`, as the rave does for a bouncer nobody answered), a status line can take its own tint and a badge beside its title (`status(..., tint, badge)`: the place in the street meet's race, "3/9"), the arrow can say its own words in place of the metres (`target_label(metres)`: "???" on the way to the rave's meeting point), and the ring can stand beside the target (`set_target(point, person, ring_at, radius)`: a kerb's ring while the arrow leads to the road). A second ring that is not the target is `set_mark(at, radius, tint)` (a door to see from down the street; `clear_target` and the end take it down), a card's buttons go one under the other with `show_card(..., life, true)`, and a card takes a colour of its own with `show_card(..., life, stacked, tint)` (the marina's card, the red of a lost race). On a card of the run the autopilot presses the button `_auto_button()` names (the first unless the script says another; -1: none). One the player flies, or leaves the car for, sets `flown` (its arrow shows in the air, and the stop assist and the autopilot's car leave its target alone); one whose own things lead the player takes the arrow down with `target_arrow()` (the boat race's rings on the water: the minimap keeps the dot); and one that starts without a call calls `start()` itself (a job the player pulls into gives its own `_idle_tick` in place of the call's clock, as the round-up does). A side job sets `is_job` (while the first minutes' guide runs its target is the main line's, and the other jobs are not told the player is away on a tour) and, for cards at the bottom of the screen, `cards_low`; one whose request is a card beside the road as the player drives past (ACCEPT / SKIP) sets `offer_busy` off, so it is on only once it is taken (the trend job), shows what it says at the end beside the road too with `show_side_card(...)` (its buttons one under the other with the last argument), and gives a caller a face that is not under their name with `_call_card`'s "face". One whose drive goes on under its cards sets `cards_hold` off (Łuber: the passenger's question on the way, the result card that goes once the car moves on): `_run_tick` runs under the card and the ✕ still ends the run; it takes a card down without a press with `close_card()` (the door reached before the answer). One that drives the autopilot's car itself on a test run gives `drive_player()` (true: the car was driven this frame; main.gd asks the activity whose target is up, after the races). One with small pictures beside its status line gives them to `status(..., icons)` (the kebab's heat, the jars that are whole). One whose cards take the M and K keys (the pad's A and X) gives `primary()` and `secondary()` (true: the key was its own; the director asks every activity in turn, `activities.primary()`, the rescue line last). Every activity says what the rescue calls do while its run is on with one field, `rescue_calls` (`RescueCalls.GO_ON`: they count on, a tour; `HOLD`: they wait and go on after it, a friend's night; `RESTART`: a call ringing at the start is put off and the count starts again at the end, a job or a race); never write `missions.paused` or call `missions.close()` from an activity. A side job is asked through the director (`activities.job_on()`: one of them is on), never by its script's name; one that points the player somewhere outside a run (the deliveries' pointer to the next stand) says so with a question of its own (`pointing()`), which main.gd shows after every activity's target. One that waits for another activity's request card beside the road (a rival's challenge waits for Łuber's and the trend job's) asks `request_up` beside `free_now` (the director answers both: `offering()`, `may_start`). One whose new target the camera should not turn to look at gives `target_source()` (the races say "race": the race is on; the default is "job"), and one whose target is the main line's at other times than a side job's (while the first minutes' guide runs) gives `main_target(guide)` (the races: during the guide, and for the final or the next rung's race; the default is `is_job and guide`). It wires itself (TD-4 b, docs/design/wiring-out-of-main.md): `install(ctx)` copies what it needs from the game's services (`game_ctx.gd`: the HUD, the wallet, the map, the traffic, the car, the flags, `vehicle`, `hour`; a field it needs and the ctx lacks joins the ctx) and returns false when it is off for the run, `setup_in(ctx)` sets it up, and `joined(ctx)` wires what must follow the join and its test starts; main.gd joins it with one line at its place in `_build_world`, `x = _join(X.new(), world)` (a third argument, its own test for everything outside the kit, only when `world_free` does not fit it: the EV tow may start from the air), never a build function of its own (step `architecture` counts the joins still wired by hand); a questline also joins with one `stories.add({...})`. Its check takes its stand-ins from `game/tools/fakes.gd`. A new **tour** is almost no code: a data file `game/data/<id>_tour.json`, one join line in `_build_world` (`_join(Tour.new("<id>"), world)`), and its test flags in `dev_flags.gd` and `tour.gd`'s `_own_flags` (`check_activity` reads every tour's data and plays it to the pay). A new **race mode** runs its race on **the race session** (`race_session.gd`, `RaceSession`: `course`, `add` each racer, `start`, every frame `advance` and `signals`, 5 Hz `rank`, `turned_back` / `hop_refused`, `end`), as `rivals.gd` and the race bench do; the mode keeps its words, cards and pay | your own `enum State`, call clock, beacon, `hud.card_pressed` listener or free test; a `busy()` added to `_free_block()` (the director answers for the kit). `missions.paused` or `missions.close()` written from an activity (its `rescue_calls` says it), or a test of the rescue line's `state` (the director's `free_now`, `call_waiting`, `calls_held`, `rescue_busy` and `rescue_aimed` answer); a race mode's own ranking, effort, plans or legs; a new `jobs.busy()` asked by name for "is a side job on?" (it is the deliveries' own now) |
| When the game offers the next thing | **the offer pacer** (TD-3): your source's times (first call, next call, gaps, hours, retry) go into `game/data/pacing.json` under your source's name with a `_note`, and your script reads them under its own names at the top (`static var FIRST_CALL: Vector2 = Pacer.span("you", "first_call")`; `num` for one number, `pair` for hours kept as an Array); an activity of the kit gets `Pacer` from `activity.gd`, a tour asks by its id. The gate stays `_free_block()` (the director's `may_start` for the kit) and `hud.call_ok()` (with the quiet time after a finish); a call your own clock rings goes through `_ring_turn()` (TD-3's variety: whoever rang last lets a waiting call go first), and an offer that does not ask the gate asks `_hushed()`; `check_pacing` fails on a number nobody asks or a script that asks for one the file lacks | a timing number as a const in your script, or a third unrelated random timer; a call by your own clock that skips `_ring_turn`; a hold of the rescue calls outside the director (`hold_calls`, or your run's `rescue_calls`) |
| A card with buttons | In an activity of the kit: `show_card` (the press comes to `_card_pressed`, and the run's clock stands while it is up). Elsewhere: `hud.show_card` (or `show_side_card`, `show_call`), then `hud.own_card(self)` and a `card_pressed(index)` method: the press comes to you alone (TD-16). A picture on the card (the race result's two routes): any `Control` with its `custom_minimum_size`, handed to `hud.set_card_art(control)` right after `show_card`; the card owns and frees it. A "no, thanks" that is not a button of its own (the choice card's just drive): `hud.set_card_close(index)` right after `show_card` puts a ✕ in the card's corner that presses `index` (it may be past the card's buttons); the next card takes it away. A card nobody owns still goes to every listener of the `card_pressed` signal, as the activities not moved yet expect | a new listener of the `card_pressed` signal; `hud.hide_card()` when the card may be somebody else's (ask `hud.card_is(self)`) |
| Something the player can do, in the QUESTS screen (a questline, a kind of side job, a First step, a collectable) | `quest_book.gd`: a questline comes in by itself from `stories.add` (`story_facts` gives its facts, main.gd `_fill_map_list` hands them over); a side job's kind is a row in `JOB_KINDS` and `JOB_COUNT` (its wallet.gd `jobs` key) with its words `job_<kind>` and `job_<kind>_about` in dialogue.json group "quest_book"; its icon a row in `ICONS` and `game/art/quest_icons/<name>.png`; a new tab a key in `TABS` and its builder. | A questline, a job's places, or anything on the minimap or full map | map-markers.md section 3: `stories.add` for a questline; its badge, a job place, a service, a "?" mark or a rival's mark is `hud.markers.put` / `put_all(owner, ...)` in `map_markers.gd` (TD-13, the registry, with a `group`; the active target is its TARGET mark, main.gd `_put_target`) | a new `set_minimap_*` setter, or writing a list another script owns |

| A target on the map (arrow, metres, pin, beacon) | In an activity of the kit: `set_target(point, person)` and `clear_target()` (TD-2); a place a job picks (a door, a kerb, a spot on a lane) passes `graph.in_play(p)` (lane_graph.gd: TARGET_PAD, 80 m inside the map's outline; check_edge E4). main.gd reads `activities.targeted()` for the arrow, the metres, the maps' pin (the registry's TARGET mark, `_put_target`) and the stop assist, and the end of the activity clears it. When the game's function gets a new argument, the stand-in gets it in the same change | a class of the check's own with `toast`, `show_card`, `say`, `ground_height`, `announce`, `play_at`, `set_stop`, `stamp` and the like (step `fakes` fails); a stand-in's function, or one a check adds to it, that takes other arguments than the game's (the same step); `shore_distance`, `in_map` or `bounds` on the flat map itself (the game asks a map whether it has them, so every check on it would play something else) |
| A new script file | a row in systems.md or city-ai.md (`check_architecture` fails otherwise) | |
| More code in `main.gd` | only the wiring (a `_build_*` line, an `activities.add`); the rest in its owner above. Step `architecture` holds `main.gd`'s lines, functions, `_build_*` lines and `flags.` reads under a band (tech-debt.md, "What is clean today") | a new private busy test (`_x_free`), a new `hud.card_pressed` listener, spoken lines in code (`dialogue.json`) |

## The shape of a new system

```gdscript
## What it is and why, the ask and date, the design doc.
extends Node3D

const Rides := preload("res://scripts/rides.gd")   # in main.gd

## Set by main.gd before setup(); never looked up by path.
var hud: Node
var wallet: Wallet
var free_now: Callable          # main.gd decides if anything else runs

func setup(world: Node3D) -> void: ...   # build once, cheap
func busy() -> bool: ...                 # true while it owns the player
func _process(dt: float) -> void: ...    # thin; decisions at 5-10 Hz
```

In main.gd: a `const` preload, a `var`, a `_build_x` that sets the references and calls `setup`,
a `LoadYield.tick`, and a test flag if needed. An activity takes its shape from the kit instead
(`activity.gd`, the first row for it above): it joins the director, and nothing is added to
`_free_block`.

## Extending the facade kit

The kit (`facade_kit.gd`, docs/design/facade-kit.md) is meant to grow. When a landmark needs a
facade element the kit lacks:

1. **A new part or style goes into the kit, in the landmark's own PR**: a `kit_*` function, or a
   value in `Win`, `Balcony`, `Attic` or `Order`. Its arguments are where, how big and which
   colours; its proportions are fixed inside it. Never a private helper in `landmarks.gd`
   (verify step `facadekit` fails it).
2. **Add it to `kit_sampler()`** so `check_mesh_quality` examines it (z-fighting, floating
   parts, normals), raise the sampler's budget on purpose, and **describe it in
   docs/design/facade-kit.md** (the check fails when the kit and the doc disagree).
3. **Prove nothing else moved**: `site_dump.gd` before and after, `tools/site_parity.py`; every
   site but the landmark's own must be `same`.
4. **A colour**: a building's own measured colour is a named constant of that building. A colour
   a second building needs joins the palette (`PAL_*`, the kit and the doc's table together).

These are decisions, not routine edits, so they go to the Opus or Fable session and to F's look:
changing a proportion of an existing part or a palette value (it moves every landmark that uses
it: before and after shots of each), and any look that would need the building shader (it has no
budget; decisions.md).

A Sonnet `object-modeler` uses the kit and escalates when a part is missing; the session that
wrote the spec adds the style.

## When the owner can't do it

1. Extend the owner in the same PR, with its check script, if the change is small.
2. Otherwise build the smallest workaround, mark it `# WORKAROUND(arch): <why>, see tech-debt TD-n`,
   and add or update the row in [tech-debt.md](tech-debt.md).
3. Say it in the PR under "Architecture": used owner X / added workaround TD-n.
