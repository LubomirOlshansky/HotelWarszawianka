# Q22 (Al. Jana Pawła II 22): spec

Site key `q22` in `game/scripts/landmarks.gd` (`TOWERS` entry, built by `_build_q22`). Written 2026-11-08 in a cloud thread from the top-50 photo set F gathered on the Mac (`warsaw-needed-photos/top-50/T02`, six Wikimedia Commons photos, reference only, never shipped): T02-01 and T02-06 the tower from Varso (south), T02-02 the crown close from Varso, T02-03 from the Palace, T02-04 the tower from Rondo ONZ, T02-05 the skyline from the west. Massing from the OpenStreetMap parts in `centrum.json`. Brand as the parody DELOJT.

## 1. Identity

1. A dark green glass shaft, five-sided, darker and greener than every tower round Rondo ONZ (T02-01, T02-05).
2. A solid graphite crown, the top four storeys, with the tenant's name in white on it (T02-02, T02-04).
3. A grey lattice mast on the roof and a red-and-white antenna over it to 195 m (T02-02, T02-04).
4. The lower wing on the south-east side, its roof one sloping plane of dark glass falling away from the shaft (T02-01, T02-04, T02-06).

## 2. Massing

From the mapped parts: the shaft is the five-sided outline (about 37 x 60 m, the narrow end north) to 155 m; the wing the south-east part to 60 m; a one- or two-storey glass base (4.3 and 7.6 m) round both. The mast stands on the mapped lattice's footprint (about (-1766, -176)).

## 3. Facade, base to top

Base: dark glass in floor bands (S_BANDS) to 4.3 to 7.6 m. Middle: the shaft and the wing in dark green glass, S_BANDS, to 141 m. Top: the crown, 141 to 155 m, graphite panels (S_DARK) 0.15 m proud of the glass, the name DELOJT on the south and east faces.

## 4. Roof

The shaft: a pale grey roof, a 1.5 m parapet, a plant box (the outline 6 m in, 3.5 m high), the lattice mast (3.6 m square, open frame) to 172 m and the antenna in five red and white pieces to 195 m with a red lamp. Three red aviation lamps on the parapet. The wing: one sloping plane of dark glass, 60 m against the shaft, 51 m at the far edge (`_tower_cut`, `cut_drop` 9 m).

## 6. Ground and night

The base and the shaft carry the car walls. At night the glass lights by the skin's rule, the crown stays dark with DELOJT burning white on it, red lamps on the roof and on the antenna's tip.

## 7. Detail level and budget

L2; budget 720 triangles in `check_mesh_quality.gd` (607 measured): plain prisms, the identity is in the colours, the crown and the mast.
