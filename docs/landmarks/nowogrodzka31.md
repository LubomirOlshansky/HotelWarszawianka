# Nowogrodzka 31 and the rooftop billboard: log

Site key `nowogrodzka31` (`landmarks.gd` `_find_nowogrodzka31`, `_build_nowogrodzka31`, `_n31_billboard`).

## 2026-11-07: built from F's four photos (cloud thread)

- Photos: the block by day from the Novotel's side, and three at night (the end wall with its banner, the
  billboard with its floodlights and beams, the whole block from the junction).
- Site: OpenStreetMap building 110 (LoD2 roof 23.4 m over the pavement). A slab 59.7 x 12.75 m along
  Marszałkowska's east side, with a wing at the south end that makes the end wall 25.7 m wide (the end
  wall faces Nowogrodzka and the Novotel). Frame origin (-692.3, 467.2), u along the slab, front = -v.
- Built: 22 bays of square windows (5 office floors and an attic row of small ones) with floor bands and a
  projecting cornice; shop panes along the front (dark, warm, pink, cool lit at night), fairy lights,
  RESTAURACJA and BUM BAR neon; two white "BIURA DO WYNAJĘCIA" banners; the end wall's dark ad banner from
  the second floor up (SZTUKA UKŁADANIA MISKI, DLA KOTÓW) with 11 lamps under its hem; the rooftop billboard
  30.7 m x 8.6 m on a steel base and stays, a cat and a bowl of food on it, the parody wordmark MRAU, 15
  floodlights on arms along its top edge and their beams (`landmarks.beams`, `map_builder.gd`
  `_build_landmark_beams`: glow.gdshader's pixel bands, night only, one mesh).
- Parodies: the cat-food brand is MRAU, the slogan is our own ("the art of arranging the bowl").
- Left alone: the balconied block beside the end wall (OSM 421, LoD2) stays as it is; the shops' names are
  generic or parodies, no real names.
- Open: the balconied block taller than the cornice in the photos; the ground-floor details beyond the first
  photo (awnings, the vertical BIM BAR sign); the billboard's exact cat and wording.
