# Nowogrodzka 31, its rooftop billboard, and Marszałkowska 99: log

Site keys `nowogrodzka31` (`landmarks.gd` `_find_nowogrodzka31`, `_build_nowogrodzka31`, `_n31_billboard`) and
`marszalkowska99` (`_find_marszalkowska99`, `_build_marszalkowska99`). Both are built in one frame: origin
(-704.5, 556.2), u south along the Parkingowa front, v east.

## 2026-11-07: built from F's photos and Google 3D views (cloud thread)

- Photos: the block by day and three at night (the end wall with its banner, the billboard and its lamps, the
  block from the junction); F's Google aerial and 3D views placing it and its neighbour.
- First pass went on the wrong block (OSM 110, north of Nowogrodzka). F corrected it: Nowogrodzka 31 is the
  office block south of Nowogrodzka between Marszałkowska and Parkingowa, its end wall facing the Novotel.
- Nowogrodzka 31: OSM buildings 445 and 444 (LoD2 roof about 24 m). A C in plan: the 78.5 m Parkingowa front,
  wings at both ends reaching west, the court open to Marszałkowska 99. 29 bays of square windows (5 office
  floors and an attic row of small ones), floor bands and a projecting cornice, shop panes (dark, warm, pink,
  cool lit at night), fairy lights, RESTAURACJA and BUM BAR neon, white "BIURA DO WYNAJĘCIA" banners. The end
  wall on Nowogrodzka: its east 11 m bare with windows, the rest under the dark ad banner from the second floor
  up (SZTUKA UKŁADANIA MISKI, DLA KOTÓW) with 11 lamps under its hem. On the roof edge the billboard, 38 m x
  8.6 m on a steel base and stays, a bowl of food and a cat on it, the parody wordmark MRAU, 15 lamps on arms
  along its top edge (the panel glows at night). No light beams in the air: the streaks in the night photos are
  the camera's (F).
- Marszałkowska 99: OSM building 5505 (LoD2 roof 27.3 m). A white slab, 8 floors over the shops: windows in
  22 bays on the Marszałkowska front with balconies in every other bay, plain windows on the court side, two
  stacks of balconies on the narrow north end, the stair tower in the court, a yellow ad banner by the north
  end.
- Parodies: the cat-food brand is MRAU, the slogan is our own.
- Open: the ground-floor details beyond the photos (awnings, the vertical BIM BAR sign); the billboard's exact
  cat and wording; Marszałkowska 99's exact balcony rhythm and shop names (from the 3D views only).
