# Plac Grzybowski block reference report (F's photos, 2026-11-08)
Files: refs/plac-grzybowski/ (D01-D12, gitignored; sources in SOURCES.md of the `pkin-south` set). Game coordinates, ±3 m. The church (Kościół Wszystkich Świętych) is `wszystkich.md` and is kept as built.

## Layout (OSM)
Water area (the fountain pool) x -1417..-1406, z -230..-201. The roads: Plac Grzybowski west leg (12 m, x ≈ -1440, running N-S), the east leg (4.5 m, along the buildings at x ≈ -1340), Próżna leaving at (-1371,-239). Tall white residential slabs: 8089/8090/8095 (49 m) south-east; 1397/1405 (41-49 m) north; a 14 m corner block (1394) on the east side.

## What the photos show
- **The square is a sunken water garden** (D01, D04, D11): **grey exposed-aggregate concrete** paths, **stepped granite terraces** (5-6 steps, 45 cm run, light grey granite), shallow pools with **dark red-brown timber (ipe) decks** and stepping stones, **timber benches with grey concrete supports** (dark red-brown slats, about 1.8 m long) along the paths, tall **ornamental grasses** and flower beds, rows of **plane/lime trees** along the east edge and the Marszałkowska side, autumn colours (D01).
- **Fountain** (D04, D11): water jets rising from the pool's edge in the terrace's step; children wade; the pool is shallow. **Kamień Pamiątkowy** (D11): a rough dark granite block about 1.2 m high on a low plinth, with a bronze plaque (the 1904 PPS demonstration).
- **Charlotte Menora café** (D08): a **glass shopfront under a white ribbon-windowed block** on the square's east side, a red-and-white fascia, **rattan bistro chairs** and round tables under the trees, a hedge between the tables and the lawn. Parody here: CHARLOTTA MENORKA.
- **White residential slabs** (D01, D07, D09): 12-14 storeys, pale plaster, regular window grids, balcony stacks on the end faces, rooftop terraces with planting; low 1930s tenement frontage beside them (D01, D09 left); a yellow ochre slab on the north-east (D01 right).
- **Car park towards Marszałkowska** (D12): a surface car park behind red-and-white bollards with a community garden strip of planters on the lawn (Ogród Społeczny na Próżnej).
- **Centralny Przystanek Historia IPN** (D10): the Marszałkowska 107 corner, grey stone ground floor with columns and a **louvred upper block** over dark glazing; it belongs to the PAST-a / Marszałkowska 107 site, not to the square.
