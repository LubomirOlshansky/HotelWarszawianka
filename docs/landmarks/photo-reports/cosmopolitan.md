## What the photos show
- **Skin**: dark blue-black float glass in a fine storey grid with black spandrel bands and **bronze vertical fins** at the corners and every few bays (C01, C02, C04, C05). Reads nearly black in sun, steel blue in the sky's reflection (#3c566e calmed).
- **The overhang's underside is cherry red** (C02, C04): the signature. A flat red soffit plate under the strip, the lower floors hanging below it in dark glass for two storeys.
- **Podium**: four storeys of **green-grey glass in dark mullions** (#6f8d89), stepped back on the corner with a **glass box** on the terrace under the overhang and a **long glass canopy** on slender grey posts over the east forecourt (C02: lattice of white-framed glass); a metal arm and a sculpture of a man on the canopy's edge; café umbrellas and planters on the forecourt; a low **black canopy** over the ground-floor shopfronts.
- **Crown** (C01, C03): the top three storeys are an **open louvred screen** (grey-bronze horizontal louvres in a white-grey frame with three vertical dividers) over glass, the roof edge a thin dark band, **plant block and a window-cleaning crane** on the roof.
- **Night** (C06): the podium glows warm; the tower stays dark with lit offices in rows (not confirmed for the whole height from the square).
