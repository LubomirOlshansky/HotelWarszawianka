- Kładka Abakanowicz, the footbridge from Karowa to Port Praski, is built from photos: the light grey deck on white piers, the wooden seating bays, the rust railings with their warm night light and the red meridian line. People sit on its steps, cyclists, scooters and walkers cross it both ways, and passers-by joke about the "bridge to nowhere".
