- The Lutheran Holy Trinity rotunda on Plac Małachowskiego is rebuilt from photos all round: its white drum banded in courses with tall arched windows and big framed round windows, four porticoes of fluted columns with a half-moon window in each pediment and a gilt glory over the doors, the low copper dome with little dormers and a taller glazed lantern with its cross.
