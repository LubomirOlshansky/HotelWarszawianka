- The Nożyk Synagogue on Twarda is rebuilt from photos all round, its front now facing the courtyard to the west: the cream rusticated front with its three-bay middle under a gable with a corbel-arch frieze, the big roundel with the Star of David, the arched door at the top of its steps with the Tablets of the Law between two little domed turrets, the urns on the pylons, the plainer wings with their triple arched windows, and behind them the nave, aisles and choir under grey roofs. The old model faced the wrong way and had a cross on its gable.
