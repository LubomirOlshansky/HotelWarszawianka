- The Baroque church of the Nativity on Leszno (al. Solidarności) is rebuilt from photos: its salmon-pink three-storey front with white pilasters, the scrolls and the pediment, the small green clock turret on top, the nave over lean-to aisles and the lower chancel under red tiles. The old model was a beige box with a tower the church never had, facing away from the avenue.
