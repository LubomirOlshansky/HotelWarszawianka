- The giant gold scissors stand on their granite plinth in front of the museum.
- TR Warszawa's site now runs almost to the Palace: a foundation slab with rebar mats, dug earth, the site offices and an excavator at the far end.
- The Christmas market is going up on Plac Defilad, south of the museum by Marszałkowska: white and dark half-timbered huts (some still bare frames or roofless, ladders, a white van), wooden planters, a picket fence, and on the lawn to the south the big tree with red and gold baubles and the Jarmark hoarding.
- Plac Centralny's ten lamps are the historic candelabras: granite columns on pedestals, five white globes each.
- The two tall granite obelisks stand on Marszałkowska at the north and south ends of Plac Defilad.
