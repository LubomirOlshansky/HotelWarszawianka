- St Charles Borromeo on Chłodna is rebuilt from photos: the cream classical basilica with two slim towers on its west front (arched belfries, octagonal lanterns, small grey domes and crosses), the tall nave with round-headed windows over the pilastered aisles and their relief panels, the transept's pediments and doors, and the round apse toward Elektoralna under a grey-green half dome. Its card no longer places it by Powązki Cemetery.
