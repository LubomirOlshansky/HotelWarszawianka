Beta guide

Welcome to the beta

Around Warsaw is a free driving game in the real centre of Warsaw. Thank you for testing it. Installing takes about two minutes.

Join on iPhone Join on Android

Free, no ads, no purchases. iPhone with iOS 16 or newer. Android 7 or newer with Vulkan 1.1.

Install

Open this page on the phone you want to play on. Your phone's steps are shown first.

iPhone: TestFlight

TestFlight is Apple's free app for testing apps before they are in the App Store.

  1. 1Install TestFlight

    Get it from the App Store. It is free and made by Apple.

  2. 2Open the beta link

    Tap Join on iPhone on this page, then View in TestFlight. Open it on the iPhone itself, not on a computer.

  3. 3Accept

    TestFlight shows Around Warsaw. Tap Accept.

  4. 4Install and play

    Tap Install, then Open. The game is on your home screen too; the orange dot by its name means it is a beta.

Updates

New builds arrive in TestFlight. On the game's page there, turn on Automatic Updates. Your progress stays when the game updates. A build stops working after 90 days, so keep it current.

Feedback from TestFlight

Take a screenshot while playing, tap the preview, then Share and Share Beta Feedback. Add a few words and send. If the game crashes, iPhone offers to send a report: tap Share.

Join on iPhone

Android: Google Play

The beta is an open test on Google Play. Use the Google account that is signed in to Play on your phone.

  1. 1Open the beta link

    Tap Join on Android on this page. If you see Become a tester, tap it.

  2. 2Install from Google Play

    Tap Install. The listing shows that you are a tester.

  3. 3Play

    Open the game from Play or from your home screen. Updates come like any other app's.

Not available?

Check which Google account is active in Play (many phones have two) and try again in a few minutes. If Play still hides the game, the phone is too old for it: the game needs Vulkan 1.1.

To leave the beta, open the testing page and tap Leave the program. Testing page

Your first ten minutes

Play the way you would at home, without reading ahead. That is the test we need most.

  1. Start a new game

    Follow the first job and tell us where you got lost or bored.

  2. Take jobs

    Deliveries and rides around the centre. Is the pace fun?

  3. Race a local

    Was it clear where to go? Did the rival feel fair?

  4. Fly the helicopter

    It unlocks after your first rescue. Fly over the Vistula at night.

  5. Change the weather

    Night and rain are in Settings. Does it still run smoothly?

  6. Close and come back

    Reopen the game later. Is your progress still there?

Known issues

No need to report these, we are on them.

  • Some buildings are still simple blocks.
  • On a few street corners the car can get caught. TOW TRUCK frees it.
  • Hot or slow phone: Settings, picture quality, choose Auto or a lower level.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. No ads and no purchases. It is a fan game about Warsaw.

Will I lose my progress?

Updates keep it. Saves live only on your phone, so deleting the game deletes them.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes, with the same TestFlight link. It is made for phones, so the screen is laid out for one.

Which languages?

English and Polish. Switch in Settings.

What data does the beta send?

Anonymous play statistics (no name, no account) and what you write in a feedback message. You can turn the statistics off in Settings.

The link does not open.

On iPhone, install TestFlight first and open the link on the phone. On Android, check the Google account in Play. Still stuck? Write through the form.
