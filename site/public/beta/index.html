    TestFlight asks nicely. Say yes.

  4. 4Install and drive

Quick questions

Is it really free?

Yes, the whole game. No ads, no purchases. The złoty in the game are fake, sorry.

Will I lose my progress?

No, updates keep it. Only deleting the game deletes it.

The link doesn't open.

iPhone: install TestFlight first and open the link on the phone, not a computer. Android: check the Google account in Play.

What does the beta send?

Anonymous play stats (no name, no account) and whatever you write to us. Stats can be switched off in Settings.
