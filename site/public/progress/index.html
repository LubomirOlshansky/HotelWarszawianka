17 November 2026

  • Varso Place now has its lower office wings round the two glass towers: eight storeys of cream stone grid, a dark set-back top floor, roof terraces with planters and small trees behind glass rails, and Varso 2's glass-roofed atrium against its tower.
  • Two new camera views in the DEV settings: a top-down view straight down over the car, north always up, in the style of the classic overhead city driving games (the view pulls back as you speed up), and an aerial view high over the city, like the website's pictures of Stadion Narodowy.
  • The website has a new look: four full-width chapters of the city, the game's own list of things to do, and one clear button to join the beta. Android shows "opening soon" with a one-email "tell me when".
  • The first start always opens at the Palace of Culture: even if you quit before your first job, the next launch still begins with the Palace flyover and the car at the start by Marszałkowska.
  • After the night club's raid on Plac Defilad the patrol cars that boxed you in now drive off by themselves, so you can leave the plaza. The map legend no longer shows more than its total ("ALL 70/62"), and the race hint no longer ends in "!.".
  • The Palace of Culture's two granite obelisks stand in their real places, one in each paved semicircle at the Palace's north and south ends on the Marszałkowska side, about 12 m tall, each with the low exit hatch from the Palace's underground beside it. One used to stand at the Patelnia and one on the park's corner, both twice too tall.
  • The north semicircle is level paving now, not a walled pit with a car park ramp and a garage door in it.
  • The phone call's Answer and Decline buttons are now named for screen readers.
  • Mirek's garage is much cheaper for the beta: paints from 50 zł, so your first delivery already buys one; tyres from 100 zł, engine from 120 zł, nitro 350 zł and the coupe 1,500 zł.
