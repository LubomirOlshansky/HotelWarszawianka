Progress · Postępy

The best of what is new in Around Warsaw, newest first. Najważniejsze nowości w Around Warsaw, od najnowszych.

23 November 2026

  • Słoik z Radomia no longer drives up a side street and back down it when his satnav fails: he takes a short way round a block.
  • Pani Halina's first race is a little harder.
  • The win card's "behind you by" now shows the rival's real gap.

22 November 2026

  • The QUESTS picture in "Things to do tonight" is a fresh shot of the current QUESTS screen: new look, icons, SHOW ON MAP.
  • Pasaż Wiecha is dressed for Christmas: shooting stars on every lamp mast, a neon gingerbread man you can walk through, a neon fir.
  • The cafés along Pasaż Wiecha have their own signs and lights now, and people stroll and take photos there.

21 November 2026

  • Trams now sometimes ring a real "dzyń dzyń" bell, rung a few times in a row, when they pull out of a stop or brake for your car.
  • Rondo de Gaulle'a as it is today: the date palm without neon on a planted island, a few deckchairs on the lawn by the de Gaulle statue.
  • Round the rondo: Vitkac with rounded granite corners and glass ribbons, BGK's roof sign, and BANK PEPEO and ŻUBR on the Party House.

20 November 2026

  • Karowa's curved stone viaduct, Wiadukt Markiewicza, is built from photos: cream arches you drive under, balustrades with obelisk lamps.
  • A small memorial to Krzysztof "Szaya" Szaykowski, the voice of Karowa's rally night, stands by the loop.
  • The driving screen, the map, the quests screen, the garage, the title screen and the clubs now share one look: dark cards.

19 November 2026

  • Zodiak's neon name and star now stand on the front of the pavilion, facing the square, not on the back, and a little bigger.
  • The glass towers at Rondo Daszyńskiego (Warsaw Spire, Skyliner, Warsaw Unit, Mennica, Generation Park Y, the HUB and The Bridge) are now dark navy.
  • Pasaż Wiecha comes alive at night: lit shop windows with shelves, striped awnings, small light-box signs on the fascias.

17 November 2026

  • Varso Place now has its lower office wings round the two glass towers: eight storeys of cream stone grid, a dark set-back top floor.
  • The full map is clean now: the city, your car, your route, your friends' quests with their names, and one big QUESTS button.
  • New QUESTS screen: first steps, friends' quests, side jobs, rivals and your collection in tabs, each with what it is, when and where.

16 November 2026

  • Żyletkowiec on Marszałkowska now has its white "razor blade" fins on every bay, a row of stone piers along the ground floor.
  • Town cars at busy junctions no longer pass through your car and then get stuck inside it.
  • The new Towarowa 22 quarter by Rondo Daszyńskiego now has paved squares and a lawn under the yellow hall frame, instead of patches of bare brown earth.

15 November 2026

  • The cream convent range on Żytnia at the Wolność corner now has its brick plinth, rusticated ground floor with barred windows, cornice and grey roof.
  • The long convent wing behind the cream range on Żytnia now matches it, with the same plaster, brick plinth.
  • Warsaw Trade Tower looks more like itself: a darker banded glass drum, warm beige stone, dark slits round its silver crown.

14 November 2026

  • Our Lady of Victory on Grochowska is the real church now: a cream-white 1930s co-cathedral with a tall front, a big round window in a yellow frame.
  • The Pentecostal church on Sienna is rebuilt from photos as the 1950s modernist hall it is.
  • The Visitationist church on Krakowskie Przedmieście is a whole new building from photos, and its front now faces the street.

13 November 2026

  • The Church of the Holiest Saviour on Plac Zbawiciela is rebuilt from photos: white walls, two slender towers with dark green spires about 70 m high.
  • Plac Zbawiciela's colonnades stand on real columns now, with the green balustrade of the terrace on top and lanterns underneath at night.
  • The slip road below the Royal Castle, from the riverside up past Pałac pod Blachą into the W-Z tunnel, is one smooth climb now: no more steps.

12 November 2026

  • During Ivan's shisha night no delivery job starts until he is home, and no other call comes while his offer or the night's last card is up.
  • Rivals in a race now drive a racing line: they brake before a bend and take it no faster than your own car could.
  • While Bartek's dead TESŁO is being towed no side job, ride or other call starts, from his offer until the car has stood ten seconds on its charger.

11 November 2026

  • The Rotunda by the Palace is brighter and more like the real one: white window frames, a blue glass eye in the roof.
  • The whole Złote Tarasy complex got its details, not just the roof: glass shops lit at street level under a glowing canopy.
  • Eight new Podwózka passengers with their own stories and places to go: a shopper to Vipkac, an artist to Zachęta.
