The real centre of Warsaw.

About 21 km², from Arkadia to Stadion Narodowy, built from open map data. If you live here, you can drive home.

Centrum

The Palace, the Rotunda, Marszałkowska. Traffic included, as always.

Stare Miasto

Sigismund's Column, the Castle, cobbles. Mind where you park: the city guards do.

Plac Zamkowy

Wisła

Fly it, sail it, or cross it at dawn. Drag to change the time; the rain is extra.

About the author

Hi, I'm Lubomir Olshansky. By day I build iOS and Kotlin Multiplatform apps at a bank in Poland, and I've lived in Warsaw for twelve years.

As a kid I dreamed of a game set in the city I live in. When I saw Isometric Warsaw and what generative AI makes possible, it finally looked doable. I want it to hold what living here is like, not just the map.

Free. No ads, no purchases, no tracking; works offline. Built in Godot from OpenStreetMap and GUGiK open data. Every brand in the game is a parody.
