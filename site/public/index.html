How it's made

Around Warsaw is a free fan game made in public. Browse the design wiki or follow what changed each day.

  • Design wiki
  • Daily progress
Licence
Source-available for non-commercial use (PolyForm Noncommercial for the code, CC BY-NC for the art and sound). The design wiki is public.
Engine
Godot 4, GDScript, the Mobile renderer. Runs at 60 fps on an iPhone 11.
Map
OpenStreetMap streets and GUGiK's 3D buildings, terrain and laser-scanned trees.
Built
Started 27 October 2026, with changes landing every day. 154 ranked places so far; 39 rebuilt from photos, 48 more from outlines.
Privacy
No ads, no purchases, no tracking. The game works offline.

How it grew so far

  1. 27 Oct

    A small car in an isometric centre of Warsaw, built from open map data.

  2. 28 Oct

    The helicopter, rescue calls, traffic and trams, day and night, the first landmarks.

  3. 29 Oct

    The city streams in pieces; a 20 km² map, the app-taxi job, the first Android build.

  4. 30 Oct

    The game becomes Around Warsaw. The city notebook, PÓŁNOC FM, 40 more landmarks.

  5. 31 Oct

    The map grows north, couriers race you at night, a vintage tram and a first garage.

  6. 1 Nov

    Parks come alive, the Vistula boulevards fill with people, and churches, Ujazdowski Castle and MDM look like themselves. The Autopilot playtester starts hunting bugs.

  7. 2 Nov

    Landmarks start to be built from real photos: Krakowskie Przedmieście, the Warsaw Rising Museum, POLIN, Most Poniatowskiego. The quest list joins the map, and gamepads work.

  8. 3 Nov

    The beta opens: TestFlight for iPhone, Play for Android. A voiced fly-over intro, Arkadia, the Forest towers, Hotel Europejski and the Daszyńskiego skyline, and bar streets that wake up after dark.

  9. 4 Nov

    Driving by buttons: steering strip and pedals by default. Rival racers drive like real ones, and a city progress console tracks every place.

About the author

Hi, I'm Lubomir Olshansky. By day I build iOS and Kotlin Multiplatform apps at a bank in Poland, and I've lived in Warsaw for twelve years.

As a kid I dreamed of a game set in the city I live in. When I saw Isometric Warsaw and what generative AI makes possible, it finally looked doable. I want it to hold what living here is like, not just the map.
